Privacy

Your journal isn't a chatbot.

Memories is built to keep the writing yours. No account. No ads. No remote AI reading your journal.

The app works offline, and private iCloud sync can keep your archive with your Apple account when enabled.

No account

Open the app and write. There is no Memories profile to create.

No ads

Your journal is not inventory for an advertising system.

No AI reading entries

Patterns are calculated from your own recorded data. Memories does not send your journal to a remote AI service.
