Where are my memories stored?

Memories keeps your journal on your iPhone. Optional private iCloud sync may be available in the shipping build after device QA; this site will only describe that once verified.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no Memories account to create.

Can Memories read my journal with AI?

No remote AI service is part of the app.

How do I restore Memories+?

Use Restore Purchases inside Memories Settings.

How do I export my memories?

Open Settings and use the export options there. Text and JSON export are available on the free tier. Memory Book PDF export is part of Memories+.

How do I delete my data?

Final deletion guidance will be published here after CloudKit / on-device deletion behavior is verified for the shipping build.
